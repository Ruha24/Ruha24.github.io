How it works

No surprises: you know what’s happening with your project at every step.

  1. Request

    Describe the task in the form or on Telegram. I reply within a day.

  2. Discussion

    I clarify the details, propose a solution, and we agree on scope and timeline.

  3. Development

    I work in stages and show you intermediate versions.

  4. Delivery

    You get the app, an installer, a user guide and the source code.

  5. Support

    I help with rollout and fix bugs if any turn up.

Frequently asked questions

Didn’t find your answer? Ask on Telegram: @ruha_42

What do you need from me to get started?

A couple of sentences is enough: what the app should do, who will use it and when you need it. You don’t need a formal spec — we’ll work out the details in a conversation.

Who owns the code?

You do. I hand over the source code together with the finished app, so any developer can keep working on it without depending on me.

Can you work on an app someone else wrote?

Yes, it’s one of my favourite kinds of work. First I go through the code and explain what’s going on in it and what can be improved, then I start making changes.

Which platforms do you build for?

Windows and Linux. A macOS build can be discussed too — Process Launcher, for example, runs on both Windows and macOS.

What if a bug turns up after delivery?

I’ll fix it. For apps with a database, the first month of support after delivery is included.
